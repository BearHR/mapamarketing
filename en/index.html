Why it works

How Google decides who shows up first on the map

Google is public about how it ranks local results: relevance, distance and prominence. It isn't magic and it isn't luck. Every task we do pushes one of those three levers.

The three factors, and how much you can move each one

Relevance

How well your business matches what the person typed.

  • Exact categories and services on your profile
  • One page for each service in each city
  • New content on what they ask

Distance

How close you are to the searcher. The one lever you don't fully control, though you can define your ground properly.

  • Service area declared correctly on all 4 maps
  • Service-and-city pages with their own content
  • Identical address everywhere

Prominence

How known you are outside your own site. This is where the map is won or lost.

  • Consistent citations across directories
  • Local links from real sites
  • Active profiles with photos and posts

And now there's a fourth door: AI

More people are asking ChatGPT, Gemini or their phone assistant for a plumber, a roofer, a landscaper. Those answers don't come from nowhere: they're assembled from map profiles, reviews, directories and websites that state clearly who you are and where you work.

The good news: it feeds on the same things we're already fixing. A business with consistent data, answered reviews and content that answers real questions is a business AI can cite with confidence.

How we use AI ourselves

We use it for the boring parts: researching what people search in your area, organizing article topics, preparing drafts, and checking consistency across profiles. It frees us up for the parts that need judgment.

What we don't do is publish generic machine output. An article that says nothing specific about your trade doesn't rank and, worse, doesn't convince anyone to call you.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?
There's a monthly fee and that's it: the website is included when you start with four months. The amount depends on how many services and how many cities you need covered, because a business with one service in one city and a business with six services in four cities are not the same job. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us your situation in two minutes, and we'll give you the exact number in that same conversation. No discovery calls, no hour-long presentations.
Can I just buy the website?
We'd rather you didn't, and here's the honest reason: a new site, however well built, takes months to rank and needs content and profiles being worked behind it. If we hand you the page and disappear, odds are you'll be in the same place six months later and conclude the site was useless. That's why we work as a program: the site is included and the monthly work is what makes it pay off. If your situation is different, message us and we'll talk it through.
How long until I see results?
Google profile work can move in weeks: categories, photos, posts and reviews show up fast. Ranking the website in search is slower, usually three to six months of consistent work, and it depends on how competitive your area is. Anyone promising you first place in 30 days is selling smoke.
I already have a website. Is it usable?
Sometimes yes. If it loads fast and you can edit it, fixing it often beats replacing it. Other times it's built on a platform that's too heavy or won't let you touch what matters, and patching it costs more. The diagnosis tells you which case you're in, and we'll tell you even when the answer means less work for us.
Do you only work with Latino businesses?
It's our specialty and where we do our best work: we understand the market, we work in both languages, and we know how a family business actually runs. It's not a closed club, but if you want a generic agency there are plenty. We're good precisely because we're focused.
Why WhatsApp only?
Because it's where you actually reply. Forms get lost, emails go to spam, and calls catch you on top of a roof. On WhatsApp you write when you can, we answer by text or voice note, and both of us have it in writing.
Do I have to sign for a year?
The initial commitment is four months, and that's what makes including the website possible. It isn't a trick to tie you down: it's the minimum window in which local work genuinely starts to show. After those four months you continue month to month. We'll walk you through the exact terms on WhatsApp before you decide anything.
What about reviews? Do you get them for me?
We don't buy or invent reviews: that can cost you the profile. What we do is set up the path (direct link, written request, the right moment to ask) and help you with the replies. The review has to come from your customer, after good work from you.
I work out of my truck, I have no storefront. Can this work?
Yes, and it's the most common case. Google has a setup for businesses that travel to customers: you declare a service area and hide your home address. It's legitimate and fully allowed, as long as it's configured correctly.
